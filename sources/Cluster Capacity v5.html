This text is temporary. We use to explain the design behind the dashboard. It will be replaced with explanation suitable for actual management of the environment.

3 trend charts are needed as capacity management has 3 dimensions.
  • Capacity: the actual consumption. It shows the counts of vSphere Clusters with Capacity Remaining (%) in red zone and orange zone. Yellow and Green zones are not shown to enable focus. They are shown in the table listing all clusters.
  • Performance Risk: Needed since you don't want performance problem. It shows the score of Cluster Performance Risk (%). We display the worst among the cluster, and the 90th percentile value. A big gap between the 2 lines indicate the problem is not widespread. Average is too late as we want all clusters to be performing. 95th percentile will not represent the whole population in clusters with many VMs
  • Performance: same with Performance Risk, but uses actual contention metrics. Needed as risk measures potential, not actual.

If you mix prod and non-prod in the same chart, expect the numbers to be dominated by non-prod. If you want to focus on prod, add a filter at the top, or simply have 2 different dashboards and open them side by side.

Due to large cluster population and 5K VM, expect the numbers to be relatively stable and matches your overall business cycles and production changes.
