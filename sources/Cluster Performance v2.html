These 3 trend charts show data from all clusters in our production environment.
Due to large cluster population, we expect the numbers to be relatively stable and matches the overall business cycles and production changes.
A performance is defined not by ESXi utilization, but by contention experienced by VMs in the cluster.
Since these are production, we expect the numbers to be in the Green Zone. The range is 75% - 100%.
A cluster that falls below the Green Zone will contributed to the count of clusters that are in Yellow or Orange or Red zone.
